DLUT Online transforms model cloud cover, wind and WMO weather codes into
procedural sky, volumetric clouds, lighting and haze effects. These visualizations do not
reproduce observed cloud shapes or campus weather-station measurements.
Free API access is for non-commercial use. Commercial deployments require an
appropriate Open-Meteo subscription: https://open-meteo.com/en/pricing
Campus query centres: http://map.dlut.edu.cn/openmap/mapi/campus/v1
Solar calculation reference: https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/calcdetails.html
